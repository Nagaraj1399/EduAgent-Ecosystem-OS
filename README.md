# EduAgent Ecosystem-OS 🚀 v3.6 Enterprise
> A Multi-Stakeholder Enterprise Education Platform powered by Google Cloud & Gemini 1.5 Pro AI Ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 Overview
**EduAgent Ecosystem-OS** is a comprehensive, AI-driven educational management and assessment platform designed to bridge the gap between **Students, Teachers, and Parents**. Moving beyond standard mock interviews, the platform delivers a multi-stakeholder ecosystem featuring real-time multimodal AI evaluations, actionable analytics, and automated progress tracking.

---

## 🎯 Core Features & Modules

### 1. Student Portal & Multi-Domain Mock Interviews
* **5 Career Track Selection Cards:** Responsive selection for Software Development / Full-Stack, Non-IT & Business Operations, Accounting & Finance, Cyber Security, and Cloud & DevOps.
* **Dynamic Context Binding:** Automatically updates target roles, technical domain topics, and candidate experience presets to pass tailored payloads to the backend `/api/ai/mock-interview` service.
* **Adaptive Persona AI Prompting (`Dr. Alex Vance`):** Dynamically adjusts interview strategies based on chosen domains (e.g., threat modeling & PKCE for Cyber Security, DCF valuation for Finance, eBPF & Kubernetes for Cloud/DevOps).
* **Robust Video & Audio Integration:** Features simulated HD candidate streams, live subtitles, voice stream audio feedback, and STAR Method AI evaluation scorecards.

### 2. Teacher Portal & Analytics
* Comprehensive tracking dashboard enabling educators to monitor student engagement, task completion rates, and technical assessment performance.
* In-depth evaluation insights, identifying missed milestones, and tracking individual student progress metrics.

### 3. Parent Portal & Progress Reporting
* Automated progress reporting bridged directly from teacher analytics and student evaluations.
* Gives parents complete visibility into student growth areas, completed tasks, and performance milestones.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express, TypeScript
* **AI & Cloud Ecosystem:** Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Google AI Studio, Gemini 1.5 Pro Multimodal API, Vertex AI integration patterns.
